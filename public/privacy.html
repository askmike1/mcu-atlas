Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

MCU Atlas is a free, fan-made tool for exploring and tracking your Marvel Cinematic Universe viewing progress. This page explains what data the app handles and how, in plain terms.

No account, no server

MCU Atlas runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server for this app, no user accounts, and no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted to us. We don't know who you are, and we don't want to.

Local storage on your device

Your watched/watching status, any custom user profiles you create in the app, and your display preferences are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device — it is never sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time using the "Reset" button in the app, or by clearing your browser's site data for this page.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to use the "Google Drive sync" feature, the app uses Google's own sign-in flow (Google Identity Services) to request access to your Google Drive. It only ever requests the restricted drive.file scope, which lets the app read and write only the single file it creates for your watch progress — it cannot see or touch anything else in your Drive.

Your synced data is stored in your own Google Drive, under your own Google account — we have no server and never receive or store a copy of it ourselves. You can disconnect access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Google's handling of your account and sign-in data is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Cookies and tracking

MCU Atlas does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Third-party links

Detail pages link out to external sites such as Wikipedia, IMDb, Disney+, and Fandom for reference. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which are not covered by this page.

Children's privacy

MCU Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and since the app collects no personal data from anyone, no data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the app changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
